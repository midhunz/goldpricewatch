Why Salalah Gold Rates Differ?

Gold rates in Salalah are influenced by international market trends, import duties, and local taxes. Local bullion associations often set the base rate used by most jewelers in the region.

Buying Tip

Always buy hallmarked gold from a licensed jeweller. 916 Gold (22K) is the most preferred for ornaments in Salalah due to its durability and resale value.

Investment Value

Gold is considered a safe-haven asset in Salalah. During economic uncertainty, gold prices tend to rise, making it a stable long-term investment for many households.
